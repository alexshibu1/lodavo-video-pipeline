# Reel Script: "What Your Lottery Money Is Actually Worth"

## Viewer lock

```
Viewer: 25-35 Canadian who buys Lotto Max tickets weekly,
        knows it's dumb but does it anyway
Wants:  to feel smart about money without being lectured
Fears:  finding out exactly how much they've wasted
Creator's link: built the calculator that does this math for you
```

## Opening A — Guilt Math (lever: specific number + shame)

> **Spoken:** "You've spent fourteen thousand dollars on lottery tickets."
>
> **First frame:** A counter rapidly ticking from $0 to $14,000 on a
> black screen, white mono text, each tick accompanied by a slot-machine
> pull sound.
>
> **On-screen text (frame 1):** `$14,000`
>
> **Seam into body:** "And your odds of getting it back?"

## Opening B — The Coin Flip (lever: physical impossibility)

> **Spoken:** "Flip a coin. Get heads. Now do it twenty-four more times
> in a row."
>
> **First frame:** A coin mid-flip, close-up, slow motion. The number
> "25" appears huge behind it.
>
> **On-screen text:** `25 heads in a row`
>
> **Seam into body:** "Those are your Lotto Max odds."

## Opening C — The Canadian (lever: scale + patriotism)

> **Spoken:** "Pick one Canadian. Any Canadian. Out of all forty-one
> million of us."
>
> **First frame:** Aerial shot of a packed stadium, then a map of
> Canada filling with dots.
>
> **On-screen text:** `1 person out of 41 million`
>
> **Seam into body:** "You just beat Lotto Max odds. Congrats."

## Shared body

> **Click confirmation (3s):**
> "Here's what OLG doesn't put on the billboard. A Lotto Max play is six
> dollars. One draw a week, fifty-two weeks — that's three hundred and
> twelve dollars a year."
>
> *On screen: the math written out, each number popping as it's said.
> Source: OLG.ca appears small in the corner.*
>
> **Value beat 1 — the 10-year number (3s):**
> "Do that for ten years, you've spent thirty-one hundred dollars."
>
> *On screen: a bar chart growing year by year, the total climbing. At
> $3,120 the bar turns red.*
>
> **Re-hook:**
> "But here's the part that actually hurts."
>
> **Value beat 2 — the savings flip (4s):**
> "Put that same six bucks a week into something earning seven percent.
> In ten years, that's forty-six hundred. In thirty years — over
> twenty-five thousand."
>
> *On screen: the red "spent" bar sits next to a green "saved" bar that
> overtakes it, then rockets past. The counter lands on $25,362. The
> green bar pulses.*
>
> **Second conflict:**
> "And you're four thousand times more likely to get struck by lightning
> than to win the jackpot."
>
> *On screen: ⚡ icon, "4,000×" in large text.*
>
> **Payoff (3s):**
> "I built a free calculator that does this math for any lottery, any
> amount, any number of years. Link's in bio."
>
> *On screen: phone mockup showing the calculator UI with a result.
> Quick interaction: user drags the years slider, numbers animate. No
> Lodavo branding visible in the mockup — just the tool.*
>
> **Earned ending:**
> "Save this for later."
>
> *On screen: "Save this ↓" — clean, no handle, no logo.*
